Yuchen (Ean) Wang

I work on agentic post-training and the systems that make it practical.

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and a research intern with Alibaba's Accio team. Previously, I studied artificial intelligence at Peking University in the Zhi Class.

My work spans long-horizon tool use, reinforcement learning, and efficient training and inference. I also study how to schedule and accelerate video generation.

Accio team · 2026

Occamy-1.0: Open Pareto-frontier 35B Intelligence for Co-work

Core contributor to a 35B-A3B agent model. I built execution-grounded data and post-training infrastructure, including verifier-gated collection, token-exact replay, and compaction-aware training traces.

The released model scores 82.2 on Claw-Eval and 27.6% on AutomationBench, up from 69.5 and 7.5% for the base model.

arXiv Website Model Code

UIUC · With Prof. Fan Lai · 2026

CineFlow: Parallel Video Generation through Semantic Dependency Scheduling

Dependency-aware video generation using semantic DAGs, runtime trajectory fusion, and critical-path scheduling—without retraining the underlying models.

1.7–5.5× end-to-end speedup and 5.4–17.3% higher VBench overall across three video models on 8×H100.

Paper Project

UIUC · Technical report · 2025–2026

Dynamic Prefill Optimization via Adaptive Online Packing

An AIMD controller uses live p95 time-to-first-token feedback to guide length-aware prompt packing before continuous batching.

Up to 20% lower TTFT on production-style DynamoLLM traces.
