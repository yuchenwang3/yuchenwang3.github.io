I'm Yuchen (Ean) Wang, an M.S. student in Computer Science at UIUC and a research intern with Alibaba Accio. I previously studied AI at Peking University in the Zhi Class.

I work on how agents learn to use tools and carry out long-horizon tasks: post-training algorithms, execution-grounded data, and the training infrastructure behind them. My systems work also spans efficient inference and parallel video generation.

Core contributor to Occamy. Contributing to vLLM, NeMo RL, Megatron-LM, and other open-source training and inference libraries.

Core contributor · Accio team · 2026

Occamy-1.0: Open Pareto-frontier 35B Intelligence for Co-work

Core contributor to Occamy-1.0 across agentic post-training algorithms, training infrastructure, and data: staged SFT/HDPO, model soup and SAO, compaction-aware credit assignment, execution-grounded data synthesis, and multi-harness asynchronous training.

The released model scores 82.2 on Claw-Eval and 27.6% on AutomationBench, up from 69.5 and 7.5% for the base model.

arXiv Website Model Code

Co-first author · UIUC · With Prof. Fan Lai · 2026

CineFlow: Parallel Video Generation through Semantic Dependency Scheduling

Dependency-aware video generation using semantic DAGs, runtime trajectory fusion, and critical-path scheduling—without retraining the underlying models.

1.7–5.5× end-to-end speedup and 5.4–17.3% higher VBench overall across three video models on 8×H100.

Manuscript Project

RareDxRL · Reasoning

Co-first author · With Prof. Tianyu Liu · 2026

RareDx: Controlled Knowledge Integration and Graph-Grounded Policy Optimization for Rare-Disease Diagnosis

Graph-grounded reinforcement learning with rewards informed by disease ontologies, biomedical similarity, and phenotype–disease links.

With SFT and retrieval, 27B diagnosis Hit@10 increased from 28.53% to 40.76%.
