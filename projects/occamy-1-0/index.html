Occamy-1.0 is a co-work model from Alibaba’s Accio Team, further trained from the post-trained Qwen3.6-35B-A3B checkpoint: 35B total parameters, with 3B active per token. It carries out workflows spanning search, tools, code, and files, with an emphasis on state tracking, recovery, and verified completion.

My contribution

I worked on execution-grounded data and post-training infrastructure:

  • Executable tasks with explicit constraints, environment fixtures, and graders; verifier, contamination, and deduplication checks before training admission.
  • Multi-harness trajectory collection with token-exact proxy/TiTO replay, environment-state reconstruction, and immutable provenance.
  • SFT-ready traces with context-segment lineage and loss masks, preserving task-level outcomes across history rewrites without double-counting tokens.
  • Matched evaluations of task success, token use, latency, and tool failures.

Training recipe

The team develops two complementary checkpoints from the same starting model. The Marathon Expert uses full-parameter SFT followed by HDPO for sustained, long-horizon execution. The Sprint Expert uses SFT on a broader distribution of shorter tool-use, coding, and search tasks. A uniform model soup merges the two, followed by SAO reinforcement learning on the merged policy’s interaction distribution. GRPO is a comparison objective, not the final post-merge training stage.

The multi-harness training infrastructure is publicly released as Dressage, covering token-exact trajectory capture, sandbox integration, and multi-segment conversion for RL. The deduplicated SFT union contains 14,998 trajectories and 403.3M tokens across general agentic work, long-horizon interaction, software engineering, and tool-call grounding.

Each row uses the benchmark’s own score or unit; these are not interchangeable accuracy measures. Native browser or desktop visual interaction is not part of the current co-work training interface.

In the matched combined Claw-Eval T/C evaluation, tokens per recorded trajectory fall from 185,999 to 149,713 (−19.5%), and trace wall time from 74.58 to 39.96 seconds (−46.4%). Timeout rate falls from 9.88% to 2.18%, and invalid-call rate from 1.85% to 0.86%. These efficiency measurements refer to that evaluation, not every benchmark or deployment.

The report also compares performance and API-equivalent cost across four benchmarks. Cost is first averaged per task within each benchmark, then equally averaged across benchmarks; 35B-A3B models use a common pricing schedule for comparison. This is a standardized cost comparison, not a measured GPU bill.
